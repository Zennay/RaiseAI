# Gemini + Google Home setup

Raise AI does **not** replace Gemini. It triggers the real Gemini / Android voice assistant that is already connected to your Google account.

## One-time phone setup

On the Android phone paired to the Galaxy Watch 7:

1. Open **Gemini** and make sure Gemini is the phone's default digital assistant.
2. Use the **same personal Google account** that has access to your Google Home.
3. In Gemini, open **Connected apps** and make sure **Google Home** is enabled.
4. Make sure **Keep Activity / Gemini Apps Activity** is enabled if Google Home linking requires it on your account.
5. Make sure your lights / plugs / thermostat / TV etc. already work in the normal Google Home app.

## One-time watch setup

1. Install/open **Gemini** on the Watch 7.
2. Complete any Gemini setup prompts on the watch and phone.
3. In Raise AI tap **Test Gemini voice**.
4. Ask a normal AI question, for example: `Wat is 7 keer 8?`
5. Tap **Test Google Home command** and say a command for a real device in your home, for example: `Zet de lampen in de woonkamer aan.`

If both work from the buttons, the account/app integration is ready. The remaining Raise AI problem is only the raise-to-mouth trigger/background launch.

## What should work through Gemini

Depending on your enabled Gemini connected apps and region/account availability, Gemini can handle normal AI questions and supported app actions. Google Home can control compatible lights, plugs, switches, climate devices, blinds, TVs/speakers and other supported smart appliances.

Some security-sensitive Home actions are intentionally restricted by Google. Raise AI does not bypass those restrictions.

## Important

Use normal **Gemini assistant**, not Gemini Live, for this project. Google documents that Google Home linked-app control is not available inside Live conversations.
